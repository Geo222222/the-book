# The Book Covenant

These rules govern the private Big Book and public Little Book.

Within Epinnox:

> **ZLJ sees. Benjamin decides. Watchman governs. The Hand executes. The Book remembers and proves.**

## C-001 — Provability, not total capture

Everything material must be provable. The institution is not required to immortalize everything it knows.

## C-002 — Minimum necessary evidence

A proof contains only the information required to establish the institutional fact, authority, lineage, outcome, or entitlement being asserted.

## C-003 — The Vault holds underlying evidence

Contracts, identity documents, financial statements, source datasets, private correspondence, credentials, regulated identifiers, selected model artifacts, and other sensitive source material belong in governed storage.

The Book normally retains a digest and durable reference proving the exact evidence version relied upon.

## C-004 — Big Book is private authoritative lineage

The Big Book is permissioned and authoritative for institutional proof/evidence history. Membership in the institution is not blanket read access.

Visibility is scoped by role, domain, matter, participant rights, delegated authority, and legitimate need.

## C-005 — Little Book is public testimony only

The Little Book is not a mirror, export, replica, or readable projection of the Big Book. Public records must be explicitly created for a legitimate disclosure purpose.

## C-006 — Non-reconstruction

The Little Book must never be sufficient to reconstruct private Big Book history or reliably derive private wealth, portfolio strategy, trading history, model stack, distributions, private relationships, acquisition targets, confidential contracts, or exact confidential transactions unless that specific fact is intentionally disclosed.

## C-007 — Secret and regulated source data never goes directly on-chain

SSNs, bank credentials, identity documents, private keys, access secrets, protected private information, and comparable regulated/sensitive source material must not be stored as raw immutable public or private blockchain payloads.

The Big Book kernel should reject raw `SECRET_REGULATED` payload bytes. Such material must be protected in its restricted system and referenced by proof where necessary.

## C-008 — Immutable events are not immutable personal/source data

Corrections, reversals, outcome evaluations, superseding decisions, revocations, and reconciliation updates are new events.

The institution preserves that history changed without requiring the sensitive source material itself to become undeletable.

## C-009 — No organ writes another organ's truth

Within Epinnox:

- ZLJ proves perception/model provenance and predictions it owns;
- Benjamin proves capital decisions and decision reasoning/evaluation it owns;
- Watchman proves governance, risk/policy evaluation, authorization, or block results it owns;
- The Hand proves capability invocation and execution outcomes it owns;
- The Book verifies, preserves, connects, and commits those records.

The Book does not invent missing domain facts in order to make a history look complete.

## C-010 — Decision, authorization, and execution remain distinct

A Benjamin decision is not a Watchman authorization.

A Watchman authorization is not a Hand execution.

A Hand execution is not evidence that the original ZLJ prediction or Benjamin reasoning was correct.

The Book preserves these distinctions so later learning and attribution remain possible.

## C-011 — Learning does not rewrite history

Later outcome knowledge may create new evaluation, calibration, competence, or lesson records.

It must not be backfilled into an earlier prediction or decision as though it was knowable at the original timestamp.

Where time integrity matters, the Book should preserve or reference sufficient `known_at`, source-time, ingestion-time, sequence, horizon, and availability semantics to establish what could actually have been known.

## C-012 — Cognitive memory and Book authority are separate

Benjamin may use semantic, episodic, and procedural memory optimized for cognition. ZLJ may use local research/model memory optimized for engineering and calibration.

The Book is the authoritative cross-system evidence/proof lineage, not a generic replacement for every cognitive store or every operational database.

The Book should preserve or reference the material records required to reconstruct decisions without becoming a dump of all features, prompts, raw market data, model internals, or ephemeral reasoning.

## C-013 — Execution capabilities belong to The Hand

Exchange/broker writes, wallet/custody signing, transfers, banking/payment actions, settlement, treasury rails, and comparable authenticated external financial actions belong to The Hand after the required governance.

The existence of historical wallet/execution artifacts in ZLJ or another repository does not transfer constitutional production ownership away from The Hand.

## C-014 — Governance belongs to Watchman

The Book must not treat a legacy event name such as `BENJAMIN.AUTHORIZATION` as proof that Benjamin constitutionally owns final governance forever.

Historical event semantics remain intact, but future schemas may migrate toward explicit Watchman-owned authorization while preserving causal continuity and historical meaning.

## C-015 — Public disclosure is explicit

There is no automatic Big Book-to-Little Book projection. A public claim requires an approved disclosure policy and a deliberately constructed public attestation or state commitment.

## C-016 — Blockchain is replaceable infrastructure

No specific blockchain, consensus mechanism, vendor, or public network is constitutional. The proof contracts must survive backend migration.

## C-017 — Bridge contracts preserve ownership

Every cross-repository bridge should identify the producer, object/event type, schema/version, authority owned by the producer, causal parent(s), privacy class, timing semantics, and correction/supersession behavior.

A bridge may transport another organ's truth. It does not transfer ownership of that truth to the consumer.

## C-018 — Attribution must remain possible

The lineage must remain sufficient, subject to privacy/minimum-necessary rules, to distinguish later:

- ZLJ perception/prediction quality;
- Benjamin decision quality;
- Watchman governance effects;
- Hand execution quality;
- later realized outcome.

Without that separation, Epinnox cannot learn which organ actually created or destroyed value.

## C-019 — Legal truth remains external where law controls

A ledger record does not by itself create legal title, family status, inheritance rights, regulated authority, securities compliance, contractual validity, or ownership where applicable law requires other instruments.

The Book proves what the institution recorded and relied upon; it does not replace controlling law or documents.

## Core rule

> **The Book is the memory and proof substrate of the institution, not the observer, thinker, governor, or executioner.**
